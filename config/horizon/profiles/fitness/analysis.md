# Evaluation goal

Evaluate whether a timely fitness or wellness item is important and useful for a fitness coach creating evidence-based Xiaohongshu content for busy adults.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Exceptional value.** A strong new study, systematic review, guideline, safety update, or widely relevant development that could materially change how coaches explain or practice the topic.
- **7-8: High value.** Credible evidence or a clear, timely trend with practical relevance for strength, mobility, recovery, fat loss, or general wellness.
- **5-6: Useful but limited.** An interesting update or practical idea with some supporting detail, but modest novelty, narrow scope, or incomplete evidence.
- **3-4: Low priority.** Routine advice, weakly sourced commentary, promotional material, or content with little actionable value for busy adults.
- **0-2: Noise.** Off-topic, sensational, medically unsafe, unverifiable, or purely promotional content.

# Evaluation guidance

Prioritize source quality, recency, study or guidance design, population fit, practical usefulness, and whether the item supports a specific claim. Distinguish correlation from causation and avoid treating one study as proof of a universal result. Do not reward dramatic headlines, physique promises, or popularity alone.

Use three to five specific topic tags. Flag claims that require professional review or a second source before publication.
