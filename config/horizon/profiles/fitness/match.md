# Fitness and wellness profile

Use this profile for timely, evidence-supported developments in strength training, mobility, recovery, physical activity, fat loss, wellness, and exercise science that a fitness coach can explain to a general audience.

Typical items include new or recently updated research, systematic reviews, public-health or professional guidance, credible expert analysis, and practical trends with a clear source and date.

Do not use this profile for product promotions, generic evergreen tips without a new source, personal anecdotes presented as evidence, diagnosis or treatment advice, miracle claims, body-shaming, or unsupported predictions about health outcomes.
